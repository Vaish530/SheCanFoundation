The Hard Truth

Dear Donor, every month, a 13-year-old girl in a small village is forced to miss five days of school. Not because she doesn't want to learn. Not because she is lazy. But because she cannot afford something as basic as a sanitary pad. She hides at home in shame, using rags, newspapers, or sand — risking her health and dignity.

  • ✦ 1 in every 5 girls in India drops out of school because of periods.
  • ✦ Over 12 crore women and girls still lack access to proper hygiene.

At She Can Foundation, we have already helped **1,20,000+ girls** with free sanitary pads, workshops, and dignity kits. But for every 1 girl we reach, 5 more are still waiting. Without you, she waits. With you, she rises.
